Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. Project6 appears as an instrument by A. E. Cobley.

Two things worth knowing:

  • In REAPER, use the VST3. REAPER never asks an Audio Unit whether it has MIDI output, so a MIDI pad’s notes have nowhere to go there. The VST3 has no such problem.
  • Apple’s AUMIDISynth goes silent at a transport loop point and stays silent until the transport is stopped. That is the synth, not Project6 — the notes reaching it are correct and keep arriving. Use a different synth if you loop.
